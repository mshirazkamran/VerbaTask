# BolDukan — Master AI Video Prompt Guide
### Optimized for Kling AI, Hailuo AI (MiniMax), and Luma Dream Machine
**Goal**: Create a cinematic 3+ minute marketing video commercial (15 sequential clips of 6–10s each) with 100% visual realism and zero policy blocks.

---

## 🛠️ Tool Quick-Start & Recommended Settings

### 1. Hailuo AI (MiniMax) — [hailuoai.video](https://hailuoai.video/)
* **Best For**: Ultra-realistic human movements, authentic Pakistani shop atmosphere, zero weird hallucinations.
* **Mode**: Text-to-Video or Image-to-Video.
* **Prompting Style**: Natural cinematic language focusing on realistic actions and lighting.

### 2. Kling AI — [klingai.com](https://klingai.com/)
* **Best For**: 66 Free daily credits, precision camera control, and Image-to-Video continuity.
* **Mode**: Image-to-Video (Recommended) or Text-to-Video (Model: Kling 1.5).
* **Settings**:
  - Aspect Ratio: `16:9`
  - Mode: `Standard` (saves credits)
  - Motion Intensity: `4` or `5` (prevents jerky movements)
  - Negative Prompt: `cartoon, 3D, CGI, blurry, distorted hands, extra fingers, text errors, low quality, morphing`

### 3. Luma Dream Machine — [lumalabs.ai/dream-machine](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine)
* **Best For**: 30 Free monthly clips, smooth camera glides (dolly push-ins, pans), and Start-Frame to End-Frame transitions.
* **Settings**: Camera Motion: `Horizontal Dolly` or `Push In`, Aspect Ratio: `16:9`.
