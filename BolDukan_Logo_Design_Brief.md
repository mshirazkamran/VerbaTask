**Prepared for**: Argon (Lead Graphic Designer)  
**Project**: BolDukan (formerly VerbaTask)

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Purpose

**BolDukan** is Pakistan's first AI voice-powered retail operating system for local kiryana stores, general stores, and retail merchants. 

Instead of typing on clumsy POS machines, shopkeepers run their entire business via **WhatsApp voice notes**:
* *"Ali moty ko 2 chawal aur 1 ghee udhaar likho"* → Logs sale, deducts inventory, updates customer Khata ledger.
* *"Maal aya 20 chini"* → Restocks inventory instantly.
* *"Ali ne 500 diye"* → Settles customer debt and confirms with a courteous audio voice note in Urdu.

### Why We Are Replacing the Old Logo
> **The Previous Logo Problem**: The old logo used a **checkbox and a tick icon** (`☑`), which was a remnant of the old "VerbaTask" to-do concept. BolDukan is **NOT** a task or to-do checklist app.
> 
> **The New Direction**: BolDukan is about **VOICE ("Bol" / بول)** and **COMMERCE / STORE ("Dukan" / دکان)**. The logo mark **must feature a microphone, acoustic sound waves, or voice note wave combined with a retail store canopy or conversational chat badge**. Under no circumstances should it include checkboxes or tick marks.

## 2. Core Visual Metaphors & Concepts

The logo consists of two harmonized elements: **The Logo Mark (Icon)** and **The Wordmark**.

### A. The Icon Mark Metaphors
Choose or synthesize one of these approved creative directions:

1. **Concept 1: The Voice Storefront (Recommended)**
   - A modern retail store awning / canopy on top ("Dukan") framing a sleek broadcast microphone or voice capsule in the center with soundwaves radiating outward ("Bol").
   - Communicates: *"Your entire shop operated by voice."*

2. **Concept 2: The Conversational Voice Badge**
   - A circular or rounded speech bubble (representing WhatsApp & customer interaction) seamlessly integrated with a microphone capsule in the center and acoustic soundwaves bursting from the top right.
   - Communicates: *"Instant voice intelligence on chat."*

3. **Concept 3: The Acoustic Retail Waves**
   - A stylized geometric letter **"B"** or shopping bag / awning formed by concentric audio waveforms and a microphone capsule.

## 3. Two-Tone Brand Architecture ("Bol" + "Dukan")

Per the brand specifications, the logo must strictly follow a **two-tone color scheme**:
* **"Bol"**: One color (Slate Ink `#0f172a` in light mode, Clean White `#f8fafc` in dark mode).
* **"Dukan"**: Vibrant Accent Color (Electric Pear `#bbf246` / `#ccf381` or Emerald Teal `#10b981` / `#1d746f`).

This two-tone rule also applies to the icon mark:
* The structural frame (speech bubble cradle or store canopy) carries the primary base color.
* The voice element (mic capsule and soundwaves) carries the vibrant accent color.

## 5. Typography Specifications

* **Typeface Family**: Geometric Grotesk Sans (e.g., **Space Grotesk**, **Inter Display**, **Outfit**, or **Satoshi**).
* **Weight**: Bold (`font-weight: 700`) or SemiBold (`font-weight: 600`).
* **Letter Spacing**: Tight tracking (`-0.03em` or `-0.02em`).
* **Casing**: Title Case: **BolDukan** (or small caps / uppercase: **BOLDUKAN**).
* **Tagline (Optional)**: *"AI Voice-Powered Retail OS"* (in medium weight, uppercase, tracked out `+0.05em`).

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

| ✅ DO | ❌ DO NOT |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DO** feature a microphone, acoustic audio waves, or soundburst. | **DO NOT** use any checkbox, checklist, or checkmark/tick (`☑`, `✓`). |
| **DO** maintain the two-tone color contrast between "Bol" and "Dukan". | **DO NOT** make "BolDukan" all one single monotonous color. |
| **DO** keep the geometry bold, clean, and legible even at 16x16 px favicon size. | **DO NOT** create intricate, overly detailed 3D gradients or photorealistic textures. |
| **DO** ensure the icon works on both dark charcoal/black and crisp white backgrounds. | **DO NOT** use thin hairline strokes that disappear on mobile screens. |
