## 1. Master Continuity Bible (Mandatory for Argon)

To ensure that 15 separate AI-generated clips look like **one continuous cinematic movie**, Argon MUST adhere to these fixed parameters in EVERY single prompt and generation step.

### A. Protagonist Lock ("Tariq Bhai")
* **Identity**: 42-year-old Pakistani Kiryana shopkeeper, warm brown skin, friendly eyes with slight smile lines, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard, welcoming demeanor.
* **Attire**: Traditional steel-grey / slate-blue cotton shalwar kameez, dark charcoal waistcoat (vest) with a clean white WhatsApp smartphone in the breast pocket.
* **Consistency Rule**: Never alter Tariq's face structure, beard length, or clothing colors between shots.

### B. Environment Lock ("Madina General Store — BolDukan Powered")
* **Setting**: A bustling Pakistani retail kiryana store in Lahore / Rawalpindi.
* **Visual Elements**: Wooden counter with a glass display top, brass weighing scale, glass jars of candies, colorful stacked shelves behind the counter with recognizable staple goods (flour sacks, Dalda ghee tins, Tapal tea boxes, Shan masala boxes, lentils in burlap sacks).
* **Lighting**: Warm ambient 3200K interior shop lights mixed with soft natural afternoon golden hour sunlight from the street entrance, transitioning to pleasant evening market ambient lights.
* **Camera Style**: Cinematic 4K, 35mm and 50mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field (f/1.8), organic subtle camera push-ins, filmic color grading, realistic textures (no CGI plastic sheen).

### C. The "End-Frame to First-Frame" Hand-off Rule (Crucial for Continuity)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Argon's Golden Workflow**:
> 1. Generate Clip 1.
> 2. Export the **exact last frame (00:12:00)** as a lossless PNG image.
> 3. Use that exported PNG as the **Starting Image / First Frame** for Clip 2 in Kling or Runway Gen-3 (Image-to-Video mode).
> 4. Repeat this chain: `Clip N (Last Frame) → Clip N+1 (First Frame)`.
> This guarantees 100% character and environmental continuity with zero sudden jumps.

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions for Argon

### Step 1: Tool Selection & Settings
* **Video Generation Tools**: Recommended **Kling AI (v1.5 / Professional Mode)** or **Runway Gen-3 Alpha**.
* **Resolution**: 1080p or 4K, 24fps or 30fps.
* **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 for YouTube / Facebook / Website banner, with a 9:16 vertical crop version for Instagram Reels / TikTok.
* **Motion Strength**: Set motion slider between `4` and `6` (avoid wild camera shaking; maintain steady, purposeful cinematic camera glides).

### Step 2: The Exact Continuity Process
1. Generate **Scene 1** using the master prompt.
2. In your video editor (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut), pause at the last frame of Scene 1 (0:13) and export a still frame as `scene_01_last_frame.png`.
3. Open Kling / Runway / Luma in **Image-to-Video** mode. Upload `scene_01_last_frame.png` as the **Start Frame**.
4. Paste the prompt for **Scene 2**. Run generation.
5. Export the last frame of Scene 2 as `scene_02_last_frame.png`, feed it into Scene 3, and repeat through Scene 15.
6. This single practice completely prevents character morphing, clothing color changes, or store background shifting!

### Step 3: Audio & Voiceover Assembly
* **Voiceover**: Record the Urdu voiceover using a warm, friendly voice artist (or high-quality ElevenLabs / Gemini voice model in Urdu).
* **Sound Effects (SFX)**:
  - Scene 1: Distant traffic, shop clamor, coin drops, paper rustling.
  - Scene 4: Crisp WhatsApp mic tap *ding*.
  - Scene 5: Thermal receipt printer whir and tear.
  - Scene 8: Clear AI voice note playback.
  - Scene 13: Chai kettle hiss and pour.
  - Scene 15: Branded musical swell and signature chime.
* **Subtitles**: Hardcode clean, modern captions (white text with subtle dark backing) in Roman Urdu and Urdu script at the bottom-center.
